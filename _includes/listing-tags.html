{%- comment -%} The topic vocabulary of a listing: every tag that appears in at least 3 of the page's entries, with its count (T-0500.1.7.4 b2: split out of listing-filter.html so a page can read the number of chips before the filter is printed). Params: entries, tag_field (optional). Sets, in the caller's scope: hl_tagarr (every tag of every entry, with repeats), hl_uniq (the distinct tags, sorted) and hl_chip_n (how many of them earn a chip). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign tagbag = "" -%} {%- for e in include.entries -%} {%- assign etags = e.tags -%} {%- if include.tag_field -%}{%- assign ov = e[include.tag_field] -%}{%- if ov -%}{%- assign etags = ov -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign joined = etags | join: "|" -%} {%- assign tagbag = tagbag | append: "|" | append: joined -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign hl_tagarr = tagbag | remove_first: "|" | split: "|" -%} {%- assign hl_uniq = hl_tagarr | uniq | sort -%} {%- assign hl_chip_n = 0 -%} {%- for u in hl_uniq -%} {%- assign cnt = 0 -%} {%- for t in hl_tagarr -%}{%- if t == u -%}{%- assign cnt = cnt | plus: 1 -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endfor -%} {%- if u != "" and cnt >= 3 -%}{%- assign hl_chip_n = hl_chip_n | plus: 1 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
