The colour comes from _data/tags.yml, the controlled vocabulary that check-tags.js enforces on the catalog, so a tag's spelling and its badge colour are decided in one place. A tag the vocabulary does not know still renders, in the default blue, rather than vanishing: the gate warns about it at commit time, which is where an unknown tag should be noticed. The lookup is exact on the canonical spelling. That is deliberate: the gate fails a catalog tag whose case or spacing drifts from the vocabulary, so the include never has to guess at a near-match. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign t = include.tag | strip -%} {%- assign _entry = site.data.tags.tags | where: "tag", t | first -%} {%- assign _color = _entry.color | default: "blue" -%} {{ t }}
