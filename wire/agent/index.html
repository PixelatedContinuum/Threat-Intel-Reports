How to use it

  • Exploited vulnerabilities (nKEVs): one line per CVE in VulnCheck or CISA KEV, dated by its latest exploitation event, with CISA KEV status, severity, EPSS and priority.
  • Significant new CVEs: one line per CVE on neither KEV list. Being listed is a selection signal, not evidence of exploitation.
  • Vendor index: every vendor with its CVEs on each list. Start here to answer "what affects vendor X".
  • Full detail by vendor name (description, affected and fixed versions, CVSS vector, SSVC, exploitation citations, public exploits, detection rules): {% for b in site.data.wire_agent_buckets %}{{ b.label | escape }}{% unless forloop.last %} · {% endunless %}{% endfor %}.
  • Machine-readable JSON with every field defined: /wire/agent/index.json (also summary.json, kevs.json, cves.json, news.json, all.json), and /llms.txt.

What the terms mean

  • VulnCheck KEV: VulnCheck has recorded exploitation of the CVE. CISA KEV: also on CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, with CISA's remediation due date.
  • EPSS: FIRST's predicted probability of exploitation in the next 30 days. SSVC: CISA's own exploitation, automatable and technical-impact decision points.
  • Priority (nKEVs): The Hunter's Ledger's patch-first score for flaws already exploited: points for a no-login network flaw, CVSS 9.0 or more, public tooling, a CISA KEV listing, a recent disclosure, and a patch-first product class. Pick (CVEs): a daily shortlist of three CVEs likely to be exploited. Both are this site's own judgment, not a vendor or government rating.
  • A missing value means no confirmed value was available, not proof of absence.
